A Note to Readers

There are many ways to learn Python.

Books, videos, courses, tutorials, and AI.

I got stuck trying to consume all of them.

This roadmap isn't meant to be the fastest path or the only path. It's simply the path that finally helped things click for me.

Read.

Predict.

Write code.

Make mistakes.

Use AI to challenge your thinking rather than replace it.

Keep moving forward.
